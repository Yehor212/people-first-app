## Context

What is the situation that requires a decision? What forces are at play? Include relevant constraints: regulatory, performance budget, team capacity, existing commitments. Link to evidence (audit docs, incident reports, data).

## Decision

One paragraph stating what we decided. Present tense. Name the specific choice.

## Alternatives Considered

For each rejected option: what it was, its tradeoffs, why we did not pick it. Include at least two alternatives even if the winner was obvious — prevents "we didn't think about that" discussions later.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what we gain
- **Negative:** what we give up / what gets harder
- **Neutral:** changes in tooling, naming, or mental model that aren't clearly good/bad

## Rollout / Migration Plan

How do we get from current state to the decided state? Is this a PR, a sprint, a phased migration? What is the rollback plan?

## Verification

How do we know the decision is working as intended? Metrics, tests, ratchet entries, user feedback signals.

- Related ADRs
- External sources (links, papers, vendor docs)
- Internal: PR numbers, issue IDs, audit sections
