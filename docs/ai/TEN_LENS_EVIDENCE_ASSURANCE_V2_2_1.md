## Purpose And Truth Boundary

This protocol expands the psychology and human-factors lens without giving an agent
clinical, research, accessibility, localization, cultural, or product-approval
authority. It also reduces repeated prompt bytes and avoidable physical specialist
invocations while preserving all ten named roles as an explicit consideration set.

The implementation can prove schema, hash, state-transition, generated-artifact,
and regression-test properties for the current repository snapshot. It cannot prove
that an installed runtime loaded the intended profiles, enforced read-only tools,
used fewer billable tokens, improved model quality, understood a person's feelings,
or achieved user acceptance. Those claims require their own current evidence.

The attached master contract and normative errata form one source. The base contract
digest above matches the digest declared by E1. The errata is authoritative wherever
the two texts differ. The non-canonical phrase `COMPLETE_WITH_ACCEPTED_RISK` is mapped
to the final E1 status `AUTHORIZED_WITH_ACCEPTED_RISK`; accepted risk never becomes
verified evidence.

## Risk Classification And Required Phases

The existing operational tiers remain useful for repository routing. The assurance
bridge maps L0 to M0, L1/L2 to M1, and L3/L4 to M2. The versioned risk registry uses
exact trigger IDs and maximum severity. M0 may use deterministic lens accounting.
M1 and M2 use evidence-first adaptive routing: all ten roles are recorded as
`SELECTED` or `EXCLUDED` with direct locators, but only selected domain owners are
physically invoked.

For an abstract request such as “fix the Settings tab”, the root coordinator first
locates the exact route, component, interaction, state, copy, platform, and current
failure evidence. A feature word alone does not select a specialist. If discovery
finds a concrete trigger, its owner becomes mandatory. If protected ambiguity remains,
`MATERIAL_AMBIGUITY` selects Roles 3 and 10 and the run cannot claim a broad PASS.
Settings discovery anchors cover all eight supported locales, including Arabic and
Hebrew; the selected corpus is still followed by exact local source inspection.

The run manifest records the routing mode, observed trigger IDs, exact participant
set, and evidence-backed consideration decision for all ten roles.
Its discovery receipt is hash-bound to the direct request, current evidence locators,
resolved scope fields, complete platform/domain matrix, and the local source-discovery
commands. Every platform/domain row includes a status, reason, and current evidence
locators. Local discovery remains self-attested until a separate trusted verifier
rechecks command provenance and scope completeness; promotion fails without it.
The validator recomputes the class and mandatory phases from the canonical trigger
rules, so a caller cannot self-declare M1 after recording a governance,
protected-surface, best-practices, or deep-audit trigger. The structural validator
cannot independently infer an omitted natural-language trigger or prove that a
caller supplied the complete current scope; therefore local self-attestation never
promotes to `GO_FOR_PROVEN_SCOPE`. M1 emotional-safety work requires Role 2
`CREATE_BRIEF` and a separate `INDEPENDENT_FINAL_REVIEW`. M1 always includes root
coordination and Role 8 closure. Each selected domain owner gets an initial and final
phase. M2 additionally requires Role 10 `PASS_A` and `PASS_B`. Role 1 is the
current root task and manifest/aggregation owner, so adaptive runs do not spend
duplicate physical invocations on Role 1 receipts.

`DEEP_AUDIT` is the explicit full-council trigger. Its adaptive plan still omits
duplicate Role 1 invocations and therefore uses 17 physical phases. The reviewable
`FIXED_FULL_TEN` rollback mode preserves the legacy exact 20-phase sequence for
M1/M2; M0 remains one deterministic QA phase because it is not a full-council run.
M0 is accepted only for one of the three canonical transformation IDs (documentation
typo without semantic change, generated count refresh, or AST-equivalent formatting).
The hard ceiling remains 20. Budget exhaustion returns `ASK` with
`BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` and aggregate `EXECUTION_INCOMPLETE`; it never silently removes
a selected mandatory phase.

## Expanded Psychology And Human-Factors Lens

Role 2 is named `User Psychology, Motivational Design, Human Factors & Emotional
Safety` and has two fresh-invocation phases.

Role 2 is selected only after discovery locates concrete agency, pressure, shame,
urgency, interruption, defaults, exit/undo, account-loss/recovery, consent,
sensitive-copy, clinical-product-claim, or inferred-user-state evidence. Generic
“Settings”, “wellbeing”, “ADHD”, or “Dopamine” wording alone initiates discovery and
does not prove that Role 2 is needed.

`CREATE_BRIEF` runs before implementation or solution selection. It separates current
observation from plausible feelings hypotheses and competing explanations. It checks
agency, choice, pressure, shame, interruption, reversibility, recovery, redress, and
non-clinical boundaries. It must propose at least one constructive alternative with
a possible benefit, possible harm, an emotional-safety success criterion, a harm or
rollback threshold, and a privacy-safe verification path. Role 9 retains ownership
of product-value success and kill criteria.

`INDEPENDENT_FINAL_REVIEW` runs after implementation and checks the original brief
against a fresh scoped snapshot, exact changes, relevant evidence, exits, undo or
recovery, monitoring, ownership, and rollback. It can clear only Role 2's owned proven
scope. Actual feelings, effectiveness, and acceptance remain `UNVERIFIED` unless an
ethical, consented, cohort-bounded human study directly supports the exact claim.
Its packet and receipt must bind the immutable `CREATE_BRIEF` receipt hash; a fresh
invocation without that parent link fails closed.

Role 2 does not own WCAG, assistive-technology behavior, readability, locale, RTL or
bidi correctness, cultural acceptance, broad product value, visual craft approval,
diagnosis, or treatment. Those questions route respectively to Role 4, Role 9, or a
qualified human authority. No agent may infer a person's mental state from journal, mood, habit,
focus, sleep, or interaction history.

## Orthogonal State Model

Every specialist receipt keeps these axes separate:

- decision: `GO`, `STOP`, or `ASK`, with a typed `ask_kind`;
- claim assurance: `VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, or
  `NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_EVIDENCE`, plus `owned` and `blocking`;
- execution: mode, state, and result;
- finding closure: open, rechecked resolution or rejection, escalation, or a verified
  authority decision;
- evidence resolution, risk decision, and waivability;
- authority authentication, authorization, qualification, scope, validity, conflict,
  and revocation.

The deterministic aggregate precedence is:

1. `INTEGRITY_FAILED`
2. `BLOCKED`
3. `NEEDS_AUTHORITY`
4. `EXECUTION_INCOMPLETE`
5. `AUTHORIZED_WITH_ACCEPTED_RISK`
6. `COMPLETE_WITH_UNVERIFIED`
7. `GO_FOR_PROVEN_SCOPE`

A verified authority may accept only a waivable residual risk within current scope.
That decision has proof effect `NONE`: it does not close an evidence gap, resolve a
finding, or widen proven scope.

An accepted-risk finding must reference the canonical hash of a supplied authority
record that is current, active, conflict-cleared, qualified for the scope, authorized
for `RISK_ACCEPTANCE`, bound to the same run, and bound to the manifest policy hash.
An arbitrary valid-looking hash fails integrity validation. These fields do not
authenticate a human or credential. Because the CLI has no external authenticated authority verifier, a syntactically
valid local authority record still returns `NEEDS_AUTHORITY`. The distinct
`AUTHORIZED_WITH_ACCEPTED_RISK` branch is reachable only through an injected trusted
runtime verifier; risk acceptance retains proof effect `NONE`.

## Evidence, Snapshots, And Invalidation

`scripts/persistent-agent-orchestra/assurance-core.mjs` provides strict validators
for role receipts, append-only finding histories, usage, authority decisions, runtime
persistence inventories, deterministic aggregation, and transitive invalidation.

Repository identity includes current Git HEAD and tracked tree, dirty and untracked
path inventories, index and worktree diff hashes, critical global inputs, diff-relevant
Git configuration, scope inventory, dependency closure, and a scoped snapshot digest.
The public snapshot object contains repository-relative paths only. A changed source,
profile, policy, schema, or upstream claim reopens every dependent closure claim.
Receipt evidence IDs and finding evidence IDs must resolve to the same hash-bound
global evidence ledger. Positive assurance requires a directly rechecked record and
an artifact hash, but the structural validator does not claim that a locator or
runtime identity is independently current. Aggregate validation requires the complete
canonical dependency set, rejects claim cycles, recomputes every declared parent and
previous-receipt hash from supplied receipt bytes, and rejects missing, duplicate,
cross-run, stale-dependency, extra-parent, and causally out-of-order records. QA closure
and Role 10 Pass B bind all earlier required phase receipts. Finding version one
must preserve the original severity and exact blocking-scope set; later narrowing
requires a recomputed full-history hash plus actual origin-role and Role 8 receipt
hashes. Cross-run ledger-root persistence still requires an independently controlled
artifact store and remains `UNVERIFIED` locally.

Scoped filesystem inventory rejects intermediate symlinks, hardlinked evidence files,
and file-identity changes observed during the read. UTF-8 byte ordering, rather than
host locale ordering, makes path inventories deterministic across supported hosts.
These checks are repository-evidence controls, not proof against an adversary with
concurrent host-level mutation capability.

Role 10 Pass B has two channels. Its decision channel accepts only a strict structured
receipt. Its audit channel is bound to hashes for original scope, Pass A, current
snapshot, receipt manifest, evidence ledger, and finding ledger. Raw reports are
omitted by default and included only when explicitly required and privacy-safe.

## Token And Context Efficiency

Generated role profiles place stable role and policy material before dynamic evidence.
Dynamic packets contain the run identity, scoped snapshot, owned question, and direct
evidence locators. The cache partition binds protocol, role, policy, and semantic
contract hashes, so changed dependencies cannot reuse stale context.

The previous ten generated profiles measured 172,099 UTF-8 bytes. The current
profiles are checked against a code-owned 172,099-byte baseline and preregistered
minimum 8 percent structural reduction.
The reduction comes from deduplicating global authority categories, shortening the
evaluation-format reminder, and keeping full source applicability in the canonical
registry and generated reference. Role ownership, hard stops, role-specific human
escalations, semantic invariants, and adaptive routing boundaries remain present.
The fresh structural check measured 148,058 bytes, a 13.9693 percent reduction.

This byte metric is not an API-token or cost claim. Adaptive routing lowers planned
physical phases for bounded tasks, but total token reduction and quality
noninferiority remain `UNVERIFIED` until paired whole-run measurements. Promotion
requires baseline/candidate measurements on
the same model and reasoning effort, representative visible and owner-controlled
holdout tasks, zero new critical misses, no regression in owned-scope decisions, and
actual exposed usage fields. Missing token or cache counters are recorded as
`UNAVAILABLE`, never estimated, and cannot pass the promotion gate. Structural tests
may use numeric schema controls only to prove gate reachability; those numbers are not
real token, cost, quality, or product evidence. The promotion validator binds both
usage ledgers and recomputes total-token change from input plus output tokens.
Cached-input, cache-write, and reasoning counters remain separate diagnostics and are
not double-counted into the total. Task, subject base commit, subject snapshot,
tool/runtime inventory, permission scope, and verification/budget plan must match.
Each arm must expose exactly one model and exactly one reasoning effort, and those
values must match across arms; a matching set with different per-phase distributions
is rejected. The preregistered floor is at least 5 percent reduction.
Each arm has its own validated run manifest, assurance-build hash, registry hash,
profile hash, and exact phase topology. The assurance-build hash identifies the
assurance implementation and is deliberately distinct from the byte-identical
subject snapshot; baseline and candidate assurance-build hashes must also be
different from each other. It is generated with `--assurance-build` from an exact
versioned inventory of the root agent instructions, Codex profile mapping, RAG
preflight and corpus manifest, eval catalog and validator, canonical registry,
generator, validator/schema code, runner, protocol documents, and all ten generated
profiles. The manifest carries that inventory and its hashes; arbitrary hashes, stale
inventories, missing artifacts, and altered artifacts fail validation or current-root
recomputation with `--validate-assurance-build`. The candidate must be adaptive and the baseline must
be the canonical `FIXED_FULL_TEN` plan; different phase counts are expected but
undeclared or unknown baseline phases fail closed.
Quality noninferiority uses a zero-degradation margin and directly rechecked
adjudication evidence. A hash-bound artifact carries paired holdout IDs, result
hashes, critical-miss and owned-scope outcomes, the evaluated margin, and an
independent adjudication receipt. A separate trusted quality verifier and the final
promotion verifier both receive the complete paired comparison rather than only an
attestation label. Referenced ledger records must be directly rechecked, typed as
`PAIRED_QUALITY_ADJUDICATION`, scoped to `quality.noninferiority`, and match the
artifact's holdout IDs and baseline/candidate result hashes; an unrelated rechecked
source file cannot satisfy this gate.

## No Fake Evidence Or Product Data

Synthetic scenarios are allowed only as isolated structural test controls, including
positive and negative controls, under `config/persistent-agent-orchestra.evals.json`.
They are not
product data, user research, runtime proof, release evidence, or production defaults.
They must never enter `src`, IndexedDB, Zustand persistence, Supabase, sync, backups,
exports, shares, analytics, Sentry, AdMob, or user-visible flows.

No raw journal, mood, habit, focus, health, account, credential, or other personal
content is required by this protocol. A sensitive run fails closed unless every
persistence channel is inventoried and sensitive data is excluded from every
unverified channel.

## Local Commands

Run the deterministic contract check:

    npm run check:ten-lens-assurance

Classify observed evidence using the default adaptive router or the M1/M2 rollback:

    node scripts/run-ten-lens-assurance.mjs --classify --trigger UI_ACCESSIBILITY_I18N
    node scripts/run-ten-lens-assurance.mjs --classify --trigger DEEP_AUDIT --routing-mode FIXED_FULL_TEN

Build a read-only scoped snapshot:

    node scripts/run-ten-lens-assurance.mjs --snapshot --scope config/persistent-agent-orchestra.json --scope scripts/persistent-agent-orchestra

Prepare an operator-assisted, privacy-classified specialist packet:

    node scripts/run-ten-lens-assurance.mjs --prepare-packet --manifest output/run-manifest.json --role-id psychology-human-factors-emotional-safety --phase CREATE_BRIEF --question "Review the bounded emotional-safety question" --input-classification REDACTED_TASK_METADATA --evidence-locator src/pages/Index.tsx:1

The same CLI validates role receipts, evidence and manifest-bound non-empty usage
ledgers, authority decisions, runtime persistence inventories, strict §10.6 assurance
reports, and full aggregate packets. The runtime adapter remains operator-assisted; profile loading and effective
permissions are not proved by generated files. Inputs must be regular
repository-relative JSON files with no duplicate keys, symlink indirection, hardlink
alias, or trailing content.

For full agent-governance changes also run the orchestra, context, no-template,
production-data-integrity, best-practices, type, lint, and security checks required by
`AGENTS.md`. A green deterministic command proves only its named scope.

## Source Applicability

- OpenAI model and prompt guidance supports keeping the same model first and testing
  leaner prompts on representative evals; published improvement ranges are directional,
  not ZenFlow proof.
- OpenAI prompt caching requires exact shared prefixes with static material first and
  dynamic material last; cached and cache-write counters must be measured where exposed.
- OpenAI evaluation guidance supports task-specific datasets, production-distribution
  coverage, logging, and human calibration. The hosted Evals platform is in deprecation
  transition, so ZenFlow's canonical artifacts remain portable and repository-local.
- OpenAI Agents SDK orchestration guidance supports deterministic code orchestration
  and parallel execution only for independent work. Usage adapters may leave fields
  unavailable. Tracing can capture sensitive inputs and outputs unless disabled.
- WHO guidance supports mental-health impact assessment, expert and lived-experience
  co-design, cultural adaptation, empowerment, monitoring, and accountable crisis
  boundaries; it does not certify this implementation.
- W3C guidance supports early and continuous user involvement, informed consent,
  participant diversity, and combining human evaluation with standards. One participant
  cannot establish universal acceptance.
- NIST AI RMF is voluntary risk-management guidance, not a certification checklist.
- OWASP agent guidance supports least privilege, bounded chains, structured validation,
  human approval for high-impact actions, and privacy-aware replay protection.
- Multi-agent research used by the registry shows that debate, majority, verbosity,
  and self-review can amplify judge bias or drift. Ten roles and agreement are therefore
  never treated as evidence by themselves.
