# Orb Design Philosophy — Glass Superformula

> "The orb is not a decoration. It is a mirror of emotional state rendered as a living glass sculpture."

---

## 1. DESIGN DNA — What Makes This Orb

### 1.1 The Shape Language: Gielis Superformula

The orb's shape is generated by the **Gielis Superformula**:

```
r(θ) = (|cos(mθ/4)|^n2 + |sin(mθ/4)|^n3)^(-1/n1)
```

- **m** = harmonic family for the silhouette (3 for low-valence pressure lenses, 5 for neutral/positive bloom)
- **n1** = depth of scallops (higher = shallower, rounder)
- **n2, n3** = tip sharpness (higher = softer tips)

**Core principle**: Negative emotions stay in the 3-lobed pressure-lens family, with depth and softness changing through `n1/n2/n3`. This keeps the canonical "very unpleasant" state as a compact triangular WebGL orb and prevents adjacent negative moods from morphing through accidental star/hexagon forms. Positive emotions move into the 5-lobed bloom family. The shape IS the emotion — not just a container for color.

| Valence | m | n1 | n2 | n3 | Character |
|---------|---|------|------|------|-----------|
| -1.0 | 3 | 0.62 | 1.50 | 1.50 | Compact triangular pressure lens — distress |
| -0.667 | 3 | 1.15 | 1.52 | 1.52 | Same-phase pressure release — anxiety |
| -0.500 | 3 | 1.55 | 1.60 | 1.60 | Softer 3-lobed lens — unpleasant |
| -0.333 | 3 | 1.90 | 1.88 | 1.88 | Near-neutral pressure lens — unease |
| 0.000 | 5 | 2.00 | 2.00 | 2.00 | Smooth centered sphere — neutral |
| +0.333 | 5 | 1.80 | 1.50 | 1.50 | Soft pentagon — ease |
| +0.667 | 5 | 1.40 | 1.35 | 1.35 | Gentle petals — joy |
| +1.0 | 5 | 1.25 | 1.30 | 1.30 | Full flower — elation |

**Pressure depth formula**: `depth = 1 - (2·(1/√2)^n2)^(-1/n1)` — controls how deeply the pressure lens is scalloped. For negative states, keep `m=3` and change depth through `n1/n2/n3`.

### 1.2 The Material: Liquid Glass

Inspired by Apple's Liquid Glass (iOS 26+, visionOS), the orb is rendered as a **transparent glass sculpture** with these optical properties:

| Property | Implementation | Why |
|----------|---------------|-----|
| **Glass transparency** | `exp(-dist²/(r²·0.18)) * 0.65 + 0.05` | Bright opaque center → almost-transparent edges = real glass depth |
| **Fresnel rim** | `pow(1-NdotV, 4.2)` mixed with white | Glass reflects more at glancing angles (Schlick's approximation) |
| **GGX specular** | Roughness `0.14-0.38`, pure white `vec3(1.0)` | Physically-based microfacet highlights — glass reflects light color, not body color |
| **Dual specular** | Key (0.80) + rim (0.45) lights | Two-point lighting creates dimensional believability |
| **Subsurface scattering** | `dot(-normal, light) * 0.22` | Light passes through translucent material — warm internal glow |
| **Depth shadow** | Band at 65-90% radius, 28% darkening | Self-shadowing in concavities — the glass has thickness |
| **Glass rim glow** | Caustique band at 70-88% radius | Light refracting through the curved glass edge |
| **Edge highlight** | `exp(-sdf²/(fw²·20)) * 0.92`, 40% white mix | Sharp bright contour — the glass rim catches light |

**The golden rule**: All reflections and highlights are WHITE or white-tinted. Glass reflects the light source color, not its own body color. This single principle is what separates "colored blob" from "glass object."

### 1.3 The Color: Flowing Iridescence

The orb is NOT a solid color. It has **3 simultaneous flowing color zones**:

1. **Base color** — from valence (purple→blue→teal→green→yellow→orange)
2. **Color 2** — hue-rotated +0.78 radians with sine oscillation
3. **Color 3** — hue-rotated -0.78 radians with cosine oscillation

These blend via spatial noise fields (`snoise(center * 1.5 + time)`) creating living, Siri-like color flow across the surface.

**Iridescence layer**: Cosine palette simulating thin-film interference (soap bubble), stronger at rim (Fresnel-masked), providing subtle spectral shifts.

### 1.4 The Rings: Concentric Glass Echoes

5 concentric rings surround and pervade the body — 2 inner (visible through glass) + 3 outer (pulsing outward):

```
Inner: 0.65R, 0.35R (inside body, alpha 0.40, 0.30 — breathe with body)
Outer: 3 rings at 33% phase intervals, born at ~1.08R, travel to ~1.63R
       Fade envelope: birth 0→8%, sustain 8→40%, fade 40→72%, gone before next arrives
       Alpha: 0.95 / 0.90 / 0.80 (slight decay per ring)
```

**Metronome principle**: Body = expression (shape/color/breath adapts to mood). Rings = heartbeat (fixed 0.15 speed, ~2.2s per ring). No valence dependency on ring speed — prevents glitch on mood switch.

**Spatial gap guarantee**: Each ring fades to zero before the next ring reaches its dying position (≥0.12R gap). The orb pulses like a heartbeat: pulse, pause, pulse, pause.

**Post-ACES composition**: Outer rings bypass ACES tone mapping as additive glow overlays. This prevents the S-curve from compressing ring brightness into the aura, keeping rings clearly visible.

**GLASS_RIM_W macro**: Each ring is a **sharp glass edge + one-sided soft glow**:
```glsl
(smoothstep(soft, 0, |sdf|) × 0.70 + exp(-max(sdf,0)² / (soft²×10)) × 0.30) × alpha
```
- The `smoothstep` creates a crisp bright core (70%)
- The `exp` adds a soft glow on the OUTSIDE only (30% — light diffracting past the edge)
- `ringFw = max(fw × 4.0, 0.008)` — wider rings with minimum floor for any DPI
- Ring softness increases with travel: `ringFw × (1 + phase × 0.5)`

## 2. THE ANIMATION SYSTEM

### 2.1 Physiological Breathing

The orb breathes using a **4-phase respiratory cycle** (not a simple sine):

```
Inhale (0-33%) → Hold (33-42%) → Exhale (42-83%) → Pause (83-100%)
```

Period adapts to valence: **8s** (anxious, v=-1) → **16s** (calm, v=+1). Organic jitter (±5% noise) prevents mechanical feel.

Body scales ±2.5% with breath. Inner rings breathe with the body (phase-offset sinusoidal). Outer rings pulse independently at fixed metronome speed — a steady heartbeat regardless of breathing rate.

### 2.2 Rotation & Flow

- Shape rotates slowly: 0.055 rad/s (anxious) → 0.015 rad/s (calm)
- Color zones flow independently of rotation (different noise seeds)
- Noise displacement: 3-octave simplex, amplitude 0.003-0.01 (barely perceptible organic life)
- Domain warp: angle warped before superformula lookup (Inigo Quilez technique) for micro-asymmetry

### 2.3 Genesis Animation

On first mount, the orb materializes with **elastic ease-out-back** over 1.5s (c1=1.70158 overshoot). Glow appears before body solidifies. Plays ONCE per browser session.

### 2.4 Reactive Touch

Touch creates an expanding ripple wave from the contact point (Gaussian ring, 0.4s lifetime). Paired with haptic feedback (hapticTap) for sensory reinforcement.

### 2.5 Shimmer Burst

Large valence changes trigger a desaturation+brighten flash (luminance boost, 40% white mix) — the glass "flashes" as emotional state shifts dramatically.

## 3. THE RENDERING PIPELINE

### 3.1 Three-Tier Progressive Enhancement

| Tier | Technology | Quality | FPS | Trigger |
|------|-----------|---------|-----|---------|
| **A** | WebGL 2.0 (GLSL 300 es) | 10/10 | 60 | Default |
| **B** | WebGL 1.0 (GLSL ES 1.0) | 10/10 | 60 | GL2 unavailable |
| **C** | Canvas 2D | 8/10 | 30 | WebGL unavailable |

All tiers share identical shape presets, color mapping, and particle system. Context loss recovery: seamless A→C→A on WebGL restore.

### 3.2 Quality Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Canvas size | 280px CSS | Large enough for detail at 2x DPR |
| DPR cap | 2.0 | Beyond 2x = battery waste, no visible gain |
| Physical pixels | 560×560 | 313,600 fragments per frame |
| Antialias (MSAA) | ON | Hardware AA for sub-pixel edges |
| GLSL precision | highp (32-bit) | Prevents banding in smooth gradients |
| Edge AA | fwidth-based, scale 1.2-1.8 | Resolution-independent soft edge |
| Tone mapping | ACES filmic | Cinematic — highlights preserve hue, don't burn white |
| Alpha | Premultiplied | Correct compositing over any background |

### 3.3 Lighting Setup

```
Key light:   (-0.4, 0.4, 1.0)  — top-left, primary
Rim light:   (0.3, -0.25, 0.8) — bottom-right, accent
View:        (0.0, 0.0, 1.0)   — straight-on camera
```

Wrap lighting adds subtle diffuse for positive emotions (0-12% wrap factor). Two-light setup creates dimensional left-bright / right-shadow asymmetry.

## 4. ATMOSPHERIC EFFECTS

### 4.1 Aura & Glow

The orb emits light into its surroundings:
- **Inner glow**: `exp(-sdf × 14) × 0.45` — tight halo at body edge
- **Aura**: `exp(-dist × 2.8) × 0.30` — medium-range ambient light
- **Bloom**: `exp(-dist × 5.0) × 0.12` — wide soft bloom

All enhanced 15% in dark mode. Organic noise on aura edge prevents circular cutoff.

### 4.2 Volumetric Light Rays

God rays radiate from behind the orb:
- 5 + 8 ray pattern (two frequencies)
- Sharpness adapts: foggy/diffuse (negative) → crisp/defined (positive)
- Noise-modulated for organic variation

### 4.3 Caustics

Internal light patterns simulating refracted light through curved glass:
- Two noise frequencies (4x, 6x spatial scale)
- Intensity 6-18% depending on valence
- Tinted by body color (40% mix with white)

### 4.4 Hope Sparkle

Even at v=-1 (maximum distress), a warm amber spark persists inside — "a light within darkness." Flickers stochastically, pulses slowly. Zero at positive valence. This is the emotional design philosophy made literal.

### 4.5 Particles

22 particle sprites orbit the body between 0.65R and 1.55R. Burst inward on touch. Contribute soft glow to final composition.

## 5. WHAT NOT TO DO — Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails |
|-------------|-------------|
| Solid opaque body | Looks like a colored blob, not glass |
| Body-colored specular highlights | Glass reflects LIGHT color (white), not its own color |
| Uniform edge thickness | Real glass has variable rim — fwidth adapts to curvature |
| Sine-wave breathing | Looks mechanical — real breath has inhale/hold/exhale/pause |
| Sharp geometric edges | Organic noise + domain warp prevent "CAD rendering" feel |
| Too much noise (>1%) | Destroys the clean glass surface — Apple uses ~0.5% body variation |
| Rings as simple circles | Must be GLASS_RIM (sharp core + one-sided glow) — lines look like wireframe |
| Audio/sound on the orb | Jarring — premium mood UIs (Apple Health) use NO audio, only visual+haptic |
| Canvas < 220px | Insufficient pixel budget for specular detail and ring clarity |
| antialias: false | Saved ~0 battery on small canvas, but introduced jagged edges on rings |

## 6. APPLE HEALTH REFERENCE POINTS

### What Apple Does (iOS 17+ State of Mind)

1. **Shape**: Multi-dimensional organic forms — petal/flower geometry that morphs with valence
2. **Material**: Glass-like translucent body with visible depth and internal light refraction
3. **Color**: Spectrum from deep indigo (Very Unpleasant) through teal to warm orange (Very Pleasant)
4. **Animation**: Gentle breathing, subtle rotation, responsive to touch
5. **Rings**: NO concentric rings in Apple's version — but we keep ours as a differentiator (user preference confirmed iteration 11)
6. **Sound**: NONE — purely visual + haptic experience
7. **Rendering**: Native Metal/SceneKit with full hardware MSAA and PBR materials

### What Apple's Liquid Glass Teaches Us (iOS 26+)

From Apple's Liquid Glass design language:

1. **Refraction**: Light bends when passing through glass — we simulate with displacement-based color flow
2. **Fresnel reflections**: Edge lighting intensifies at glancing angles — our `pow(1-NdotV, 4.2)`
3. **Specular rim highlights**: White directional streaks responding to surface normals — our dual GGX
4. **Chromatic dispersion**: Prismatic color separation on edges — our iridescence layer
5. **Background integration**: Glass shows what's behind it — our transparency gradient (center opaque → edge transparent)

## 7. MODIFICATION PROTOCOL

When modifying the orb, follow these rules:

0. **Canonical orb invariant**: The product has one orb family only.
   - Large V2 orbs and every mini-orb must route through `ValenceOrb`/`MiniValenceOrb`.
   - Default visible renderer policy is WebGL. Canvas 2D is only a fallback for unavailable or failed WebGL, not a redesign target.
   - Low-valence states must stay in the `m=3` pressure-lens family. `дуже неприємно` is the compact triangular WebGL pressure lens; do not replace it with octagrams, heptagons, hexagons, SVG blobs, or a separate mini-orb shader.
   - Mini-orbs are scaled versions of the same canonical pipeline. If a surface needs a small orb, compose `MiniValenceOrb`; do not create a new visual implementation.
   - Desktop, phone, Android, iOS, and GitHub Pages must show the same canonical shape language. Validate phone and desktop screenshots before calling a visual change done.

1. **Three-tier sync**: Every visual change must be applied to ALL THREE renderers:
   - `orbShader.frag` (WebGL production)
   - `orbShader.preview.frag` (GLSL Canvas preview)
   - `orbRenderer.ts` (Canvas 2D fallback)

2. **Parameter philosophy**: Change NUMBERS, not ARCHITECTURE. The rendering pipeline is stable — tune constants, don't restructure the shader.

3. **Glass-first testing**: After any change, ask: "Does this still look like GLASS?" If the answer is ambiguous, the change is wrong.

4. **A/B with user**: The user has strong visual opinions (iteration 11: rings removed → immediately reverted). Always show before committing to design changes.

5. **Haptics stay, audio goes**: Touch feedback via `hapticTap`/`hapticMedium` is correct. Audio drone was removed (iteration 12) — do not re-add.
