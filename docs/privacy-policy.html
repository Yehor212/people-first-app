ZenFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Summary: ZenFlow is local-first. Optional account sync, private journal search, AI Coach, feedback, crash diagnostics, ads after consent, journal audio recording, and journal dictation require clear user action or consent. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Introduction

ZenFlow ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal wellness and habit tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2.2 Data We Do NOT Use for Ads or Analytics

ZenFlow does not use these sensitive app contents for ads or analytics:

  • Journal text, journal audio recordings, gratitude text, or private notes
  • Habit names, reminder text, or user-written focus notes
  • Precise location data
  • Health Connect data for advertising

Optional sync, diagnostics, notifications, and ads can involve external services only when the relevant feature, platform build, and consent setting allow it.

2.3 Microphone, journal recordings, and dictation

Journal voice recording uses your microphone only after you start recording. Recordings become journal audio attachments and may be included in optional account sync. ZenFlow does not listen in the background.

Optional journal dictation starts only when you choose the dictation control. It uses the speech recognition service provided by your browser or operating system; depending on that service and its settings, spoken audio may be processed outside ZenFlow. The resulting text is handled as journal text. ZenFlow does not save the recognition audio as a journal attachment unless you separately start voice recording.

3. How Your Data is Stored

ZenFlow is local-first. Core wellness data starts on your device, and optional services process only the data needed for the feature you enable.

  • Local app data works without an internet connection
  • Optional Supabase sync can store selected account data so it is available across devices
  • Optional diagnostics, notifications, Health Connect, and Google Mobile Ads use their own privacy controls and consent settings
  • Clearing browser/app data or uninstalling can remove local data from this device

4. Data Security

Security depends on the storage surface in use:

  • Local data is protected by your device's security measures
  • We recommend using device lock (PIN, fingerprint, face recognition)
  • Optional network services use HTTPS/TLS and provider controls appropriate to the enabled feature
  • ZenFlow does not send journal text, gratitude text, habit names, or focus notes to ad networks

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Cloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable cloud sync, your data is stored using Supabase (supabase.com), a secure cloud platform:

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Your email is used only for authentication
  • You can delete all cloud data at any time from Settings
  • Cloud sync is completely optional - the app works fully offline

Health data is processed locally on your device. If you enable cloud sync, health-derived insights may be included in your synced data. We do not share health data with third parties.

5.3 Google Sign-In (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive only your email address and profile name for authentication purposes. We do not access your contacts, calendar, or other Google data unless you explicitly enable Google Calendar integration.

5.4 Banner Ads and Google Mobile Ads

Supported ads-enabled Android builds may show one bottom banner ad after Google consent allows ad requests. The banner stays outside active journaling, mood check-ins, focus moments, and other private or emotional input flows. This release path uses the banner format only; it does not use video-for-reward or full-screen interstitial formats. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, user product interactions such as ad views and taps, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers such as Android Advertising ID or app set ID for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Where Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) requires it, ZenFlow shows a Google ad privacy choices entry point in Settings so you can change or withdraw ad consent.

5.5 Journal AI Search (Optional)

Journal AI Search is off until you choose to enable it and accept its in-app privacy notice. While it is on, ZenFlow sends your search query to a Supabase Edge Function. The function matches it against the title, content, mood, and tags of entries in your ZenFlow account.

The current lexical search path does not send journal text or search queries to an external AI provider and does not create a separate search index.

5.6 AI Coach (Optional)

AI Coach is used only when you choose to send it a message. ZenFlow sends your message, recent conversation history, and selected wellness context to a Supabase Edge Function to generate a Coach Lite response. The current Coach Lite mode does not use an external AI provider. Do not use AI Coach for emergencies or medical advice.

5.7 Feedback (Optional)

When you submit Feedback, ZenFlow sends your category, message, optional email address, app version, and device information to Supabase. Where the feedback email service is configured, ZenFlow also attempts to send a notification email containing that information through Resend. Do not include journal text, urgent safety information, or medical information in Feedback.

6. Children's Privacy

ZenFlow is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app without parental consent. The app confirms user age during onboarding.

7. Health Disclaimer

Important: ZenFlow is designed for general wellness and self-improvement purposes only. It is not a medical device or mental health treatment tool. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have mental health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

8. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app
  • Export: You can view all data stored in app settings
  • Delete: Clear app data or uninstall to remove all information
  • Portability: Data stays on your device under your control
  • Ad consent: Review Google ad privacy choices in Settings when Google requires a privacy-options entry point
