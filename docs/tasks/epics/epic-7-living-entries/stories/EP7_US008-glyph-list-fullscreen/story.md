# EP7_US008: Glyph in Journal List & Fullscreen View

**Epic:** 7 — Living Entries & Arousal Foundation
**Priority:** P2 (Medium)
**Complexity:** Medium
**Status:** Backlog
**Created:** 2026-04-14

---

## 1. User Story Statement

**As a** journaler browsing my diary entries,
**I want** to see glyph thumbnails in my entry list and tap them for a fullscreen view,
**So that** I can browse my visual diary and appreciate each entry's unique glyph.

---

## 2. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC1:** Given I view the journal entry list, when entries have generated glyphs, then glyph thumbnails (24px) appear in place of mood dots
- **AC2:** Given I tap a glyph thumbnail, when the fullscreen view opens, then the glyph displays at full size with slow rotation
- **AC3:** Given I am in fullscreen glyph view, when I tap back or swipe down, then the view closes and returns to the journal list

---

## 3. Technical Notes

### Architecture

- Modify `JournalEntryCard.tsx` — replace mood dot with `EmotionalGlyph` component at 24px
- Glyph loads from IndexedDB cache (generated by US004's `useGlyphGenerator`)
- Fullscreen view: new modal following modal-standard (fixed inset-0, z-[60], md:max-w-2xl)
- Fullscreen glyph: render at original 128px with CSS `animation: rotate 30s linear infinite`
- Android back handler required per modal-standard
- Lazy load: show placeholder shimmer until glyph loads from cache

### Standards Research

- Deterministic visual hashing ensures glyphs are always reproducible
- Full research: `docs/research/rsh-004-living-entries-standards.md` §3

### Key Files

- `src/components/diary/JournalEntryCard.tsx` (modify — glyph thumbnail)
- `src/components/diary/EmotionalGlyph.tsx` (modify — add fullscreen mode)
- `src/components/diary/GlyphFullscreenModal.tsx` (new — fullscreen view)

## 7. Design Notes

The fullscreen view should feel contemplative — dark background, centered glyph, slow rotation. Like holding a gemstone up to the light. The transition from thumbnail to fullscreen should feel like "zooming in" on the entry's emotional essence.
