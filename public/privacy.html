ZenFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Summary: ZenFlow is local-first. Optional account sync, crash diagnostics, ads after consent, and journal audio recording require clear user action or consent. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data You Create

ZenFlow stores mood entries, habits, focus sessions, journal entries, gratitude entries, reminders, and app settings on your device first. If you sign in and enable sync, selected app data can sync through Supabase so it is available on your devices.

2. Microphone and journal audio recordings

Journal voice recording uses your microphone only after you start recording. Recordings are used to create a journal audio attachment. ZenFlow does not listen in the background. If sync is enabled, journal audio attachments may be uploaded to the configured Supabase storage bucket as part of your synced journal data.

3. App Audio and Audio comfort feedback

App ambience and sound effects are local app assets. Audio comfort feedback is structured feedback such as too loud, distracting, did not play, comfortable, or prefer silent. It does not include journal text, mood labels, raw audio recordings, user IDs, email addresses, or free text by default.

4. Optional Services

  • Supabase: authentication, optional sync, and storage for synced user content.
  • Sentry: crash and error diagnostics. Error reports must not include journal text, raw audio payloads, or unnecessary personal content.
  • AdMob: optional rewarded ads after consent on supported app builds. Ads are not shown inside active journaling, mood check-in, or focus moments.
  • Firebase/Google services: may support analytics, crash diagnostics, notifications, or ads depending on platform build and consent settings.

5. Analytics, Ads, and Consent

No-tracking mode disables optional analytics and ad consent. Analytics events, when enabled, should use privacy-safe fields and avoid user-written content. Advertising ID and ad-services declarations are tied to ads-enabled Android builds.

6. Data Safety Notes for Store Review

Store forms must match the actual artifact submitted for review. If the artifact includes microphone recording, Supabase sync, AdMob, Sentry, Firebase, or journal audio storage, those surfaces must be reflected in Data safety and privacy declarations.

7. Your Choices

  • Use ZenFlow without signing in for local-first use.
  • Turn off app sound, ambience, completion cues, reminder cues, or milestone cues in Settings.
  • Disable no-tracking exceptions by enabling no-tracking mode.
  • Export or delete your data from Settings where supported.
  • Revoke microphone permission in your browser or operating system settings.

8. Health Disclaimer

ZenFlow is for general wellness and self-reflection. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice.
