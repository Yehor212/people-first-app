## Claim rejection rules

- A green focused test proves only its named contract.
- Static source, build success, subagent summaries, prior CI, and this table do not prove runtime or production behavior.
- A receipt from another commit or platform is rejected.
- The real-account removal remains user-executed and requires just-in-time confirmation after deployment.
