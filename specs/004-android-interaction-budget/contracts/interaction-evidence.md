# Interaction evidence contract
A verified Android claim needs one source/build/device lineage. Independently read installed APK hashes before and after and match both to the built APK. Repeating one argument is insufficient.
Measure activation→first visible response, activation→destination ready and activation→final transition frame separately. Report p50/p95/p99/max, counts, missing samples and clock/method. DOM/rAF is diagnostic, not screen presentation.
Phone PASS requires a physical device, production-equivalent build, exact package/version/WebView, actual refresh rate/thermal context, three warmed runs and ten worst-flow cycles; normal/reduced × first/warm × all five destinations; rapid/cancel/Back/resume and separate video/frame-deadline evidence.
Missing, malformed, non-finite, negative, mismatched or unbound evidence cannot produce PASS. Captures preserve app data, existing forwards/logcat buffers and earlier reports.
